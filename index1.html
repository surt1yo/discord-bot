Online Safety

Ding Ding! A message suddenly appears on Mila's tablet while playing Roblox, she doesnt recognize the name. She clicks the notification and reads it out loud, "Hey Mila, it's Maya. We met in a game yesterday!" Mila hesitates for a moment. The name seems familiar, so she replies. "Oh yeah, hi Maya!" The two begin chatting. Maya seems friendly and quickly asks if they can play together again sometime. As the conversation continues, Maya starts asking personal questions. "Which school do you go to?" Maya asks. Mila answers. "What suburb do you live in?" Again, Mila answers. Soon, Maya asks for her home address so she can supposedly send a gift. Although Mila feels unsure, she eventually shares the information.

After that, Maya suddenly stops replying. An hour passes. Then another. Mila sends several messages, but none are answered. That evening, while Mila is downstairs watching TV, she hears a loud noise coming from outside. Her stomach drops, she immediately sprints up stairs to check but only sees grey smoke flooding everywhere before getting hit in the head and blacking out.

Gasp, Mila wakes up in the middle of a basement, it's dark, she's tied up with ropes on a chair, unable to move, she screams for help, but her screams are muffled, her mouth covered by tape. Somebody walks in, it's her stepdad, James.

James walks over and removes the tape from her mouth slowly, enjoying every second. Mila spits on him, enraged, screaming "Why have you done this?! Why?!" James replies with, "You've always been my fantasy ever since I married your mother, and today, it ends." James suddenly pulls out a knife and rubs it on Mila's thigh, slicing her skin open. Mila screams in agony until a flashbang gets thrown inside, dozens of officers barge in, a officer shoots his gun into James's back, taking control of the situation,

One of the officers rushed to Mila's side, cutting through the ropes and pulling her from the chair. Trembling, she clung to him as he spoke into his radio. "Control, 7 Adam 100. Hostage secured. Suspect detained. Code 4."

As officers dragged James toward the exit, he suddenly stopped struggling and turned his head toward Mila. A chilling smile slowly spread across his face. "You think this is over?" he whispered. Mila froze. James let out a quiet laugh as officers forced him forward. "I'll be waiting. No matter where you go... I'll find you." The basement fell silent as he disappeared up the stairs, leaving Mila staring into the darkness where he had been standing only moments before.
