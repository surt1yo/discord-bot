How to stay safe from online strangers

Introduction:

Whenever you go online, safety is the main thing you should focus on while browsing, using social media, messaging apps, and more. This is because there are many online strangers and predators who may try to harm you or steal your information while hiding under a disguise behind the screen.


Risks Online:

There are dozens of risks online, such as cyberbullying, identity theft, and more. People may ask for your information such as your name, age, location, and more. These predators may use this information to harm you, steal your identity or impersonate someone you know to gain your trust.


Safety Tips:

To stay safe from online strangers, you should never share your personal information with anyone online, especially if you don't know them. You should also be cautious when accepting friend requests or messages from people you don't know. Always remember to use strong passwords and keep them private.


Conclusion:

By following these safety tips, you can protect yourself from online strangers and enjoy a safer internet experience.
